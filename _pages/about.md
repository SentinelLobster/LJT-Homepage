# About Me
My name is Li Jun-Ting (short name LJT). I am a third-year PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, supervised by Professor Wei Li in the Natural Language Processing (NLP) Lab. My research interests focus on deep learning, large language model (LLM) alignment, low‑resource language modeling, and multimodal foundation models.

## Academic Background
- **2023‑Present**: PhD in Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China
- **2021‑2023**: Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China (GPA: 3.95/4.0, graduated with distinction)
- **2017‑2021**: Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science, Peking University, Beijing, China (GPA: 3.82/4.0)

## Research Experience
1. **Research Assistant, Tsinghua NLP Lab (2021‑Present)**
   - Led the project of instruction tuning for low‑resource Chinese dialect LLMs, built a high‑quality instruction dataset with over 50k samples for Shanghainese and Cantonese, and fine‑tuned LLaMA‑2‑70B‑Chat for dialect understanding and generation.
   - Collaborated with researchers from Microsoft Research on multimodal alignment research, designed a cross‑modal contrastive learning approach to enhance the visual‑text reasoning ability of open‑source LLMs.
   - Implemented efficient parameter‑efficient fine‑tuning (PEFT) methods (LoRA, Q‑LoRA, IA3) for large‑scale model adaptation and released open‑source code on GitHub.
2. **Summer Research Intern, ByteDance AI‑Lab (June‑August 2022)**
   - Participated in the development of the ByteDance Seed‑LLM, focused on optimizing long‑context window processing using sparse attention mechanisms, reduced inference latency by 25% for 32k‑context inputs.
